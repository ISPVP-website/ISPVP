## Session Framework

The scientific program features four sessions held across Wednesday and Thursday. Session introductions and presentations will connect pathogen biology, plant responses, vector systems, and practical disease-management strategies.

<div class="grid two">
  <article class="card"><h3>Session 1</h3><p><strong>Vector Biology, Epidemiology, and Diagnostics.</strong> Research on insect vectors, disease spread, surveillance, and rapid detection tools.</p></article>
  <article class="card"><h3>Session 2</h3><p><strong>Molecular Host–Pathogen Interactions and Biotechnology.</strong> Molecular mechanisms of infection and immunity, microbiome interactions, and biotechnology-based approaches.</p></article>
  <article class="card"><h3>Session 3</h3><p><strong>Pathogenesis, Plant Physiology, and Disease Tolerance.</strong> Pathogen colonization, plant physiology, host responses, and mechanisms of tolerance to vascular diseases.</p></article>
  <article class="card"><h3>Session 4</h3><p><strong>Integrated Disease and Pest Management Strategies.</strong> Integrated approaches for managing vascular diseases and their insect vectors, including horticultural practices and therapeutic interventions.</p></article>
</div>

## Program Format

- Four scientific sessions held Wednesday and Thursday.
- Keynote, invited oral, selected oral, and poster presentations.
- Poster viewing and a networking reception on Wednesday afternoon.
- Networking and a closing dinner on Thursday afternoon and evening.
